Research thesis October 2026

Advancing agents
through game worlds.

World Labs is a research lab focused on long-horizon agents in interactive worlds.

Game development demands sustained reasoning across code, spatial structure, and runtime behavior. Decisions carry consequences across many steps. A feature is complete when it works in the running world and preserves the systems around it.

We are building original training data and game-engine RL gyms to advance this capability, with a research loop that measures what agents learn.

A continuous supply of original data.

We are building a continuing supply of fresh, high-quality data for increasingly difficult game-development tasks. The datasets span engine code, 3D scenes, and complete project artifacts, with technical correctness, novelty, and depth as core requirements.

We are developing these datasets for pretraining and continued pretraining. Their value is measured by the capabilities they teach.

Game engines as RL environments.

Each gym is an executable problem set: a versioned starting project, a task specification, engine tools, and verifiers for the resulting state and behavior. Agents modify code and scenes, observe runtime consequences, and receive reward tied to task completion and preservation of existing functionality.

Our direction spans environments such as Roblox Studio, Unreal Engine, and Minecraft. Tasks extend from individual mechanics to interacting systems that require planning, debugging, and recovery over long horizons.

Measure the effect of the data.

Our research loop centres on controlled training runs. Starting from the same checkpoint, we compare a current data mix with one that includes a new batch, holding the training recipe, compute, and evaluation budget constant.

Both models are evaluated on held-out gym projects, separate from training tasks. We measure completion, regressions, and recovery. The results inform the next dataset and environment release.

Data and environments for frontier labs.

Our aim is to make sustained, reliable work across interactive systems a trainable and measurable capability.
