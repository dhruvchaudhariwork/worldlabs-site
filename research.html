Research

Game craft, turned into data.

Expert gaming datasets start with a clear brief. We shape prompts, examples, critiques, and comparisons around what your team wants its model to learn.

Our approach

The right expertise.
The right kind of data.

01

Author

Write a complete prompt or create an example that captures the intended game experience.

02

Evaluate

Review an output against the brief. Explain what works, what falls short, and how to improve it.

03

Compare

Choose between alternatives for the same brief and explain the tradeoffs behind the choice.

Quality depends on the game.

A tactical RPG and an arcade fighter need different combat. A cozy sim and a survival game need different economies. The genre, audience, and design goals give each judgment its context.

The evidence matters, too. Resource tables can reveal a broken crafting loop; judging control feel needs a playable build. Each project defines what experts review and the criteria they use.

Initial projects are in development. Dataset format, review criteria, and intended use are agreed for each project.
