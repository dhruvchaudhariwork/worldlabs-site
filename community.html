04 / Questions

Before you apply.

What does the work actually look like?
You might write a game-design prompt, critique a generated economy, or compare two combat systems. Other projects could involve creating an example or revising a level. Each brief will explain the task and what to submit.
Are projects paid?
We are planning paid projects and sample assignments. You will receive the rate, scope, and payment timing before accepting work. We are collecting interest now, so applying does not guarantee an assignment.
What's the time commitment?
It will vary by project. Each invitation will include the expected time and deadline so you can decide whether it fits your schedule.
Do I need AI experience?
No. We are looking for shipped game experience and the ability to explain your decisions. Your specialty matters more than familiarity with AI tools.
How will my contributions be used?
Projects are intended to create data for AI training or evaluation. Before you accept a project, we will agree how your work may be used, whether you receive attribution, and any confidentiality terms.
